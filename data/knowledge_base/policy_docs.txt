RETAILIQ INTERNAL POLICY DOCUMENTATION
(Synthetic, for project demonstration only)

1. INVENTORY POLICY
Target inventory cover is 3 weeks of forecasted demand for A-type stores, 4 weeks for
B-type stores, and 5 weeks for C-type stores, reflecting differing replenishment lead
times. Reorder is triggered automatically when projected cover falls below 1.5 weeks.
Safety stock is set at 20% of the 8-week rolling average weekly sales for each
store-department combination.

2. MARKDOWN RULES
Markdown activation follows a staged schedule: Stage 1 (10% off) begins if a
store-department's weekly sales fall more than 25% below its 12-week rolling average
for two consecutive weeks. Stage 2 (25% off) begins after four consecutive weeks below
target. Stage 3 (40% clearance) applies to slow-moving stock older than 90 days.
Markdown decisions must be reviewed weekly against the promotional calendar to avoid
overlapping discounts with planned holiday promotions.

3. SUPPLIER TERMS
Standard supplier payment terms are net-30 from delivery confirmation. Suppliers
contributing more than 15% of a category's volume are classified as strategic and
receive a dedicated quarterly business review. Late deliveries beyond 5 business days
trigger a formal service-level notice.

4. RETURNS POLICY
Customer returns are accepted within 30 days of purchase with a receipt, or 14 days
without one, at store manager discretion. Returned inventory is inspected within 48
hours: resellable stock is returned to shelf, damaged stock is written off against the
store's monthly shrinkage allowance. Negative weekly sales figures in the sales system
reflect return volume exceeding new sales in a given week and are a normal, expected
pattern rather than a data error.

5. STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP)
Weekly sales reporting closes every Sunday at midnight, with dashboards refreshed by
Tuesday morning. Store managers must reconcile any variance greater than 5% between
system-recorded sales and register totals within 3 business days. Holiday-week staffing
must be confirmed two weeks in advance, referencing the promotional calendar and the
prior year's same-week sales as a baseline.
